\subsubsection{Goals}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \text{[G1]} The company aims to spread the project proposals to all interested students.
    \item \text{[G2]} The company aims to select the most suitable and compatible students for each project. %specify
    \item \text{[G3]} The student would like to find proposals that are most suited to him. %specify
    \item \text{[G4]} The student and the company would like to receive suggestions on how to improve CVs and project descriptions.
    \item \text{[G5]} The university would like to monitor the status of internships involving all its students and suspend them if necessary.%delete
    \item \text{[G6]} The company wants to keep track of the status of each open selection process.
    %\item \text{[G7]} The company wishes to automate all phases that do not require an human interaction (such as the creation of virtual rooms for interviews).%add our assumption
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{World phenomena}
\begin{enumerate}[label={[WP\arabic*]}, align=left, leftmargin=0pt, labelindent=\parindent, listparindent=\parindent, labelwidth=0pt, itemindent=*]
    \item The student writes his CV.
    \item The company considers a new project to propose to students and therefore it defines an accurate and clear description including also terms and requirements for the internship.
    \item The student works on the tasks assigned by the company.
    \item The company makes an interview to the student.%add specification "Interviews may be online/presence. If online, there are supported by an external tool."
    \item The external statistical analysis tool calculates statistics which will be used for recommendation process.
    \item The company prepares a structured questionnaire that will be filled out by the interviewers.
    \item The university handles the complaints of enrolled students (in order to terminate internships if necessary).%specify

\end{enumerate}
