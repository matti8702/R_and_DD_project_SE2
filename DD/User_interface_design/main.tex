\subsection{Registration and Login Page}

The registration and login page marks the first interaction with the user. In this initial phase, after 
selecting their user type, the user is required to complete all fields to complete the registration process.
Subsequently, to log in, users will only need to enter their credentials.\\\\

\subsection{Home Page}

The homepage serves as the main page of the platform. On this page, students can view and 
interact with their recommended internship offers and match proposals. Similarly, company members 
can see and interact with student CVs recommended to their company, as well as the match proposals they are involved in. 
Both students and company members can also view a reminder with their scheduled interviews information. 
University members, on the other hand, have access only to the complaints assigned to them. \\\\

\subsection{Activity Page}
The activity page allows users to monitor the status of ongoing internships. Students can write reports 
and submit complaints. Company members can also write reports and manage the selection process for 
internships that have not yet started. University members primarily use this page to oversee internships 
led by the students under their supervision. They can also interrupt an internship following a student's
 complaint if necessary.
For example, in the first case, a university member can interrupt the internship; in the second case, 
the student has submitted complaints, but all are marked as "handled," so the university member requires
 a new complaint to potentially interrupt; in the last case, the student has not submitted any 
 complaints. \\\\

\subsection{Profile Page}

The profile page allows users to view their personal information. Students can view and edit their 
digitalized curriculum or upload a new version of their CV file. Company members use this page to create
 new internship offers and update existing ones.\\\\
